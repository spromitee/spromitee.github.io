Getting started
Weigh food and portions for the first 1–2 weeks, then switch to portion control by estimation. Drink water before meals. 30 minutes of light activity each day (e.g. walking).
This is a sample weekly plan, not medical advice. Talk to a doctor or a registered dietitian before starting a weight-loss plan, and get any symptoms checked first.
