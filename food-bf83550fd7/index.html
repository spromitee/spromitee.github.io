The golden rule: sequence your plate

  1. Fiber first — korola (bitter melon), dherosh (okra), begun / sim bhaji, or fresh shosha (cucumber). Coats the stomach and slows gastric emptying.
  2. Protein next (~30g) — 2 pieces chicken, beef or duck, or 1 large fish steak + 1–2 eggs, before touching starch.
  3. Smart carb last (≤30g at dinner) — 50/50 cauli–lal chal rice, besan / almond roti, or konjac rice soaked in your remaining jhol.

The habit shift: instead of mashing rice, bhaji and jhol together from bite #1, eat half your bhaji and one piece of fish or meat plain first — then mix the rest.

Same curry, different order: eating fiber → protein → smart carb can mean up to a 70% smaller blood-sugar spike than starting with a white-rice mound.

The 50/50 trick: grate phulkopi, dry-roast it in a hot wide pan for 4 minutes, then fold 1 cup roasted cauliflower (~5g carbs) with 1/3 cup cooked lal chal (~15g). Pour hot jhol over it — the texture is virtually indistinguishable from full white rice.

Keep the curry — just change the base

Hit ~30g protein per meal

  • 2 generous curry pieces of chicken, beef or duck (~4.5 oz cooked) — ~32g
  • 1 large fish piece (rui / ilish / salmon) + 2 eggs — ~32g
  • 1 large fish piece + 1/2 cup thick masoor dal — ~30g
  • 3 whole eggs + 2 egg whites + 1 besan roti — ~31g

Ingredient matrix: load up, enjoy, tweak

Load up freely — fiber heroes

  • Korola: bitter melon, cook without potato.
  • Dherosh: okra — soluble fiber slows sugar absorption.
  • Begun, phulkopi & sim: roast or make vorta with mustard oil, onion & chili.
  • Lau, palong & kacha shosha: bottle gourd, leafy shaak, raw cucumber before every meal.

Enjoy generously — proteins

  • All Deshi maach: rui, katla, pabda, ilish, chingri, tilapia.
  • Murgi & dim: skinless chicken kosha, boiled / poached / curried eggs.
  • Haash & lean beef: zero-carb protein — skim excess oil off bhuna gravies.
  • Tofu / paneer / tok doi: unsweetened yogurt or tok doi as a high-protein side.

Smart tweaks — watch carbs

  • Skip aloo inside curries: potato spikes glucose just like white rice.
  • Misti kumra & sweet corn: keep minimal; swap pepe or mula into curries.
  • Dal counts toward both: 1 cup thick dal adds ~20–25g carbs.
  • Measure cooking oil: 1–2 tbsp mustard, avocado or olive oil instead of heavy oil or ghee.

3 roti secrets (so low-carb rotis never crack or turn stiff)

  1. Isabgol magic: 1 tbsp psyllium husk + hot water mimics gluten elasticity.
  2. Parchment roll: roll almond or lentil dough between 2 sheets of parchment so it doesn't stick.
  3. Damp cloth: move cooked rotis into a towel-lined casserole right off the tawa so steam keeps them pillowy soft.

Too busy to roll dough tonight?

  • Flame-puffed tortilla: toss a store-bought high-fiber tortilla (4–5g net carbs) straight onto the gas burner for 4 seconds per side with tongs — it chars and puffs like a homemade chapati.
  • Easy 50/50 atta + besan mix: half regular whole-wheat atta, half besan, 1 tsp isabgol. Rolls effortlessly while cutting the glucose spike.

Getting started
Eat fiber first, then protein, then carbs — especially at dinner. Keep the curries you love; only the base changes. Weigh food and portions for the first 1–2 weeks, then switch to portion control by estimation. Drink water before meals.
